# Veridia University Academic Programs

## Computer Science Department

The Computer Science program at Veridia University offers comprehensive training in modern software development and computer science principles.

### Bachelor of Science in Computer Science
- Duration: 4 years
- Prerequisites: High school diploma with mathematics and physics
- Tuition: $12,000 per semester
- Credits required: 120

### Master of Science in Computer Science  
- Duration: 2 years
- Prerequisites: Bachelor's degree in CS or related field
- Tuition: $15,000 per semester
- Thesis required

## Business School

The Business School provides cutting-edge business education with a focus on practical skills and industry applications.

### MBA Program
- Full-time: 2 years
- Part-time: 3 years  
- Tuition: $25,000 per year
- GMAT score required: minimum 600

### Bachelor of Business Administration
- Duration: 4 years
- Focus areas: Finance, Marketing, Management
- Tuition: $10,000 per semester
- Internship opportunities available

## Engineering Department

Our Engineering programs combine theoretical knowledge with hands-on laboratory experience.

### Mechanical Engineering
- Bachelor's program: 4 years
- Tuition: $11,000 per semester
- Lab facilities: Advanced machine shop, CAD lab
- Job placement rate: 98%

## Admission Requirements

### General Requirements
- Completed application form
- Official transcripts
- Personal statement (500 words)
- Two recommendation letters
- Application fee: $50

### International Students
- TOEFL score: minimum 80
- IELTS score: minimum 6.5
- Financial documentation
- Visa requirements
