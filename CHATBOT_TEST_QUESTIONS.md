# Top 7 Questions to Ask the Veridia University Chatbot

## 🎓 Academic Programs & Courses

### 1. **"What programs does Veridia University offer?"**
- **Purpose**: Get comprehensive information about all available academic programs
- **Expected Response**: Detailed list of programs including Computer Science, Engineering, Business Administration, Medicine, Arts, and Science
- **Follow-up**: Ask about specific program details, duration, and requirements

### 2. **"What are the admission requirements for Computer Science?"**
- **Purpose**: Understand specific program prerequisites and eligibility criteria
- **Expected Response**: GPA requirements, prerequisite courses, standardized test scores, and application deadlines
- **Follow-up**: Ask about application process, required documents, and interview process

## 💰 Financial Information

### 3. **"How much does the MBA program cost?"**
- **Purpose**: Get detailed financial information for business programs
- **Expected Response**: Tuition fees, additional costs, payment options, and financial aid availability
- **Follow-up**: Ask about scholarships, payment plans, and financial assistance programs

### 4. **"What scholarships are available for international students?"**
- **Purpose**: Explore financial aid options for international applicants
- **Expected Response**: Merit-based scholarships, need-based aid, international student grants, and application requirements
- **Follow-up**: Ask about eligibility criteria, application deadlines, and renewal requirements

## 📅 Important Dates & Deadlines

### 5. **"What are the application deadlines for fall 2024?"**
- **Purpose**: Get critical timeline information for application submission
- **Expected Response**: Application deadlines, document submission dates, decision notification timeline, and enrollment deadlines
- **Follow-up**: Ask about early decision options, rolling admissions, and late application policies

## 🏫 Campus Life & Facilities

### 6. **"What facilities and resources are available on campus?"**
- **Purpose**: Learn about campus infrastructure and student support services
- **Expected Response**: Library resources, laboratory facilities, student centers, recreational facilities, and technology resources
- **Follow-up**: Ask about housing options, dining facilities, and transportation services

## 📞 Contact & Support

### 7. **"How can I contact the admissions office?"**
- **Purpose**: Get direct contact information for further assistance
- **Expected Response**: Phone numbers, email addresses, office hours, location, and preferred contact methods
- **Follow-up**: Ask about appointment scheduling, virtual consultation options, and response time expectations
